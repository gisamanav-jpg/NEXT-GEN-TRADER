Privacy Notice

Demo website notice

This version of Next Gen Trader is a front-end demonstration. Its demo account and learning progress are stored in your browser using local storage.

What this means

  • Demo account information is not automatically sent to a server.
  • Browser data may be lost when local site data is cleared.
  • Data in this browser does not automatically follow you to another device.
  • Do not enter real passwords or sensitive personal information.

Future improvements

A production version will need properly configured authentication, secure data storage, and a clear privacy policy explaining any information collected and how it is used.
