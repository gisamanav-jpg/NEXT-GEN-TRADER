About Next Gen Trader

A learning project focused on understanding financial markets responsibly.

Our purpose

Next Gen Trader aims to make trading concepts easier to understand through structured courses, practical exercises and learning resources. Our focus is education, not promises of quick money.

Our approach

Students are encouraged to learn market fundamentals, examine evidence, understand risk and practise with simulations before considering any financial decisions.

Privacy Notice

This prototype stores demonstration accounts, applications and progress in your browser's local storage. That information may be visible to someone using the same browser profile and can be removed by clearing site data.

Do not submit sensitive personal information or real passwords to this demo. A production website needs a clear privacy policy, secure authentication, appropriate data access controls and a defined retention process.

When backend services are connected, this notice must be updated to explain what information is collected, where it is stored and how students can request access or deletion.

Trading Education Disclaimer

All content is provided for educational and informational purposes. It is not personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Financial markets are risky. Prices can move against expectations, and losses may exceed the amount initially expected in some products. No lesson, chart pattern or strategy guarantees a profit.

Study carefully, check information independently and consider using a simulation before making financial decisions. Never risk money needed for essential expenses.
